\subsection{Audit Scope and Trading Freshness Standards}

To reduce temporal mismatch and apply a consistent rule, share prices were observed on or after the nine-month reporting deadline mandated by Section 59(2) of the Banks and Financial Institutions Act, 2017 (BAFIA), operationalized at the end of Chaitra (mid-April). Table~\ref{tab:app_price_verification} compares the secondary market closing price recorded in the analysis dataset against the Last Traded Price (LTP) archived by the Merolagani aggregator.

The price verification confirms exact alignment between the primary ShareSansar records and Merolagani archives across 29 of the 30 bank-years. The NPR 1.00 variance for NMB in FY 2076/77 reflects a minor intraday reporting difference resolved in favor of the primary exchange close. In addition, the two excluded observations (HBL and NIMB in FY 2077/78) were confirmed to have had trading halted for 54 and 47 days respectively following post-merger regulatory procedures. These trading halts placed the earliest observable transactions outside the five-day statutory freshness window, validating their exclusion to prevent temporal aggregation bias.
