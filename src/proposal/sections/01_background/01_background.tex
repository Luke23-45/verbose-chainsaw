\section{Background of the study}

Equity valuation links share prices to expectations about future cash flows and
the information available about a firm. The residual-income valuation framework
developed by \citet{ohlson1995} represents equity value as current book value
plus the present value of expected future residual income, establishing earnings
per share (EPS) and book value per share (BVPS) as central inputs to accounting
valuation models.

In Nepal, the organized stock market grew out of the Securities Exchange Centre,
which was later restructured into Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) Limited. Trading
on NEPSE began on 13 January 1994, and it remains the country's sole stock
exchange \citep{nepse2017}. Previous research has examined associations between
accounting fundamentals and share prices in emerging markets generally
\citep{sharma2011,collins1997} and Nepalese commercial banks specifically
\citep{bhattarai2014,pradhan2016,gurungdahal2025}.

Nepal's commercial banking sector has been reshaped by mergers and
acquisitions, including several that began joint operation in early 2023. The
Nepal Rastra Bank licensed-institution list records these post-merger operation
dates and lists 20 commercial banks as of mid-October 2023
\citep{nrb2023list}. This consolidation provides a setting in which to examine
whether the associations between accounting fundamentals and market valuation
differed across the pre-consolidation and post-consolidation periods.

Much of the Nepalese share-price literature estimates average associations over
its observation period, including pooled regressions and panel models with
constant accounting slopes \citep{bhattarai2014,chhetri2023,joshi2023,pant2024,
chaudhari2025,gurungdahal2025}. Merger studies in Nepal have also examined bank
financial performance around mergers using pre- and post-merger comparisons and
more recent comparative designs
\citep{adhikari2023,adhikari2023cbr,acharya2026merger}. The studies reviewed for
this proposal do
not test whether the EPS--price and BVPS--price associations changed across the
early-2023 consolidation period. This proposal examines that specific question
using a standardized price window operationalized on or after the statutory
nine-month reporting deadline in Section 59(2) of the Banks and Financial
Institutions Act \citep{bafia2017}. Because the legal deadline does not establish
when each bank actually released its audited statements, the measure is treated
as a timing proxy rather than a verified post-disclosure price.
