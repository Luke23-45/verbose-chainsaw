\section{Background of the study}

Equity valuation links share prices to expectations about future cash flows and
the information available about a firm. Accounting earnings, book values, and
dividends are common inputs to valuation models \citep{miller1961,ohlson1995}.

In Nepal, the organized stock market grew out of the Securities Exchange Centre,
which was later restructured into Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) Limited. Trading
on NEPSE began on 13 January 1994, and it remains the country's sole stock
exchange \citep{nepse2017}.

Equity returns depend on firm performance and share price movements shaped by
technical, fundamental, and market factors \citep{bhattarai2014}. Among
firm-specific accounting measures, earnings per share (EPS), dividend per share
(DPS), book value per share (BVPS), and return on assets (ROA) have been
studied in relation to share prices in both emerging markets generally
\citep{sharma2011} and Nepalese commercial banks specifically
\citep{bhattarai2014,pradhan2016}.

Nepal's commercial banking sector has been reshaped by mergers and
acquisitions, including several that began joint operation in early 2023. The
Nepal Rastra Bank licensed-institution list records these post-merger operation
dates and lists 20 commercial banks as of mid-October 2023
\citep{nrb2023list}. Adhikari, Kavanagh, and Hampson (2023), studying seven
commercial banks involved in merger and acquisition transactions from 2013 to
2020, found that mean market price per share fell by 46.01\% in a three-year
pre-post comparison. That result applies
to the sampled earlier mergers, not to the early-2023 consolidation
\citep{adhikari2023}. Recent Nepalese studies also report mixed results for ROA
and BVPS, including studies whose observation periods extend through FY 2023/24
\citep{chhetri2023,gyawali2022,joshi2023,pant2024,chaudhari2025,timalsina2026}.

This proposal examines associations between accounting fundamentals and
post-disclosure prices over FY 2076/77 to FY 2081/82. It does not estimate the
causal effect of the mergers or test whether coefficients changed across
pre-merger and post-merger periods.
