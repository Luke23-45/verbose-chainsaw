\section{Objectives of the study}

The general objective is to examine the value relevance of accounting
information and assess whether its relationship with market prices differs
before and after the banking consolidation wave, using five purposively selected
commercial bank series over FY 2076/77--2081/82.

The specific objectives are:
{\renewcommand{\theenumi}{\Roman{enumi}}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\Roman*.,align=right,leftmargin=44.2pt,labelwidth=30pt,labelsep=14.2pt]
  \item To estimate the association of earnings per share and book value per share with market prices measured at a standardized post-reporting-deadline window.
  \item To test whether the estimated EPS--price and BVPS--price associations differ between the pre-consolidation (FY 2076/77--2078/79) and post-consolidation (FY 2079/80--2081/82) periods.
  \item To evaluate the robustness of the results using alternative accounting specifications (operating profitability and eligible dividends) and treatment of the consolidation transition year.
\end{enumerate}
}
