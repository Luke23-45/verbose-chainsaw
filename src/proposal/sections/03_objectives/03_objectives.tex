\section{Objectives of the study}

The primary objective of this study is to examine the association between
selected firm-specific accounting fundamentals and market price per share among
Nepalese commercial banks during the period spanning the major 2023
banking-sector consolidation. To achieve this primary aim, the study sets forth
the following specific objectives:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item To examine the relationship between firm-specific accounting fundamentals
    (EPS, DPS, BVPS, and ROA) and the market price per share (MPS) of Nepalese
    commercial banks.
  \item To analyze the extent to which an empirical model incorporating EPS, DPS,
    BVPS, and ROA accounts for variation in market price per share (MPS) among
    Nepalese commercial banks during the period spanning the 2023 consolidation.
\end{enumerate}
