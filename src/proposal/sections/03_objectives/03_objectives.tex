\section{Objectives of the study}

The primary objective of this study is to examine the associations between
selected firm-specific accounting fundamentals and post-disclosure market price
per share ($\mathit{MPS}^{post}$) among Nepalese commercial banks across a six-year
period spanning before, during, and after the major 2023 consolidation. To achieve
this primary aim, the study sets forth the following specific objectives:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item To examine the relationships between firm-specific accounting fundamentals
    (EPS, DPS, BVPS, and ROA) and post-disclosure market price per share
    ($\mathit{MPS}^{post}$) of Nepalese commercial banks.
  \item To analyze the extent to which an empirical model incorporating EPS, DPS,
    BVPS, and ROA accounts for variation in post-disclosure market price per
    share ($\mathit{MPS}^{post}$) among Nepalese commercial banks across the period
    spanning before, during, and after the major 2023 consolidation.
\end{enumerate}
