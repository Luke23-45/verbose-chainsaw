\section{Objectives of the study}

The primary objective is to describe and estimate the associations between
selected accounting fundamentals and post-disclosure market price per share
($\mathit{MPS}^{post}$) for up to five purposively selected Nepalese
commercial-bank series over FY 2076/77--2081/82. The analysis spans the
early-2023 consolidation period but does not estimate a merger effect or test
for a structural break. The specific objectives are:
{\renewcommand{\theenumi}{\Roman{enumi}}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\Roman*.,align=right,leftmargin=44.2pt,labelwidth=30pt,labelsep=14.2pt]
  \item To describe levels and fiscal-year patterns in EPS, DPS, BVPS, ROA, and the post-disclosure share price in the sampled bank series.
  \item To estimate the bivariate association of each accounting fundamental with the post-disclosure share price.
  \item To estimate the joint and conditional associations of EPS, DPS, BVPS, and ROA with $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$ using the stated regression model.
\end{enumerate}
}
