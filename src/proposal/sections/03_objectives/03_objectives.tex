\section{Objectives of the study}

The major objective of this study is to examine the fundamental determinants of
share prices of Nepalese commercial banks in the post-consolidation period. The
specific objectives are as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item To analyze the relationship between EPS, DPS, P/E ratio, BVPS, ROA and MPS.
  \item To assess the effect of EPS, DPS, P/E ratio, BVPS and ROA on MPS.
\end{enumerate}
