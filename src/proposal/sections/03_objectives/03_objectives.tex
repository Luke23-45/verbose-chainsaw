\section{Objectives of the study}

The primary objective of this study is to examine the associations between
selected firm-specific accounting fundamentals and post-disclosure market price
per share ($\mathit{MPS}^{post}$) among Nepalese commercial banks across a six-year
period spanning before, during, and after the major 2023 consolidation. To
achieve this primary aim, the study sets forth the following specific
objectives:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item To describe the distributional characteristics, baseline levels, and
    historical variations of EPS, DPS, BVPS, ROA, and $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$
    across the sampled commercial banks.
  \item To evaluate the direction and degree of bivariate linear association
    between each individual accounting fundamental and $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$.
  \item To estimate the joint explanatory power and conditional associations of
    the four accounting fundamentals with $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$ using multiple
    linear regression.
\end{enumerate}
