\section{Research methodology}

\msub{1.1.1 Research design}
The study employs descriptive and causal-comparative research designs. The
descriptive design establishes the distributional characteristics of bank
fundamentals. The causal-comparative design tests the effects of EPS, DPS,
P/E, BVPS and ROA on MPS.

\msub{1.1.2 Population and sampling procedure}
The population comprises all 20 commercial banks operating in Nepal as of
mid-2023, following the consolidation that reduced the count from 32 in 2014.
The sample consists of 5 banks selected by convenience sampling, identified
under their current legal names:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Global IME Bank Limited (absorbing Bank of Kathmandu, joint operation from 9 January 2023, swap 1:1)
  \item Himalayan Bank Limited (acquiring Civil Bank, joint operation from 24 February 2023, swap 100:80.28)
  \item Kumari Bank Limited (absorbing Nepal Credit and Commerce Bank, joint operation from 1 January 2023, swap 1:1)
  \item Nepal Investment Mega Bank Limited (merger of Nepal Investment Bank and Mega Bank, joint operation from 11 January 2023, swap 100:90)
  \item NMB Bank Limited (no merger in the period)
\end{enumerate}
For fiscal years preceding each merger, observations use the acquiring bank's
audited annual-report series.
Study period: FY 2076/77 to FY 2081/82 (6 years $\times$ 5 banks = 30
observations).

\msub{1.1.3 Nature and sources of data collection}
Secondary data: EPS, DPS, P/E, BVPS, ROA, MPS from Banking and Financial
Statistics (NRB), NEPSE trading reports, Economic Survey (MoF), and annual
reports of the sampled banks.

\msub{1.1.4 Research framework and definition of variables}
Dependent variable: MPS. Independent variables: EPS, DPS, P/E, BVPS, ROA.

\msub{1.1.5 Methods of analysis}
\begin{equation}
MPS_{it} = \alpha + \beta_1 EPS_{it} + \beta_2 DPS_{it} + \beta_3 PE_{it} + \beta_4 BVPS_{it} + \beta_5 ROA_{it} + e_{it}
\label{eq:main}
\end{equation}

Here MPS denotes market price per share. EPS, DPS, P/E, BVPS and ROA are
earnings per share, dividend per share, the price-earnings ratio, book value
per share and return on assets. Subscripts $i$ and $t$ index bank and fiscal
year. The intercept is $\alpha$. Slope coefficients $\beta_1$ through
$\beta_5$ capture marginal effects. The term $e$ is the stochastic error.

Descriptive statistics (mean, SD, min, max), Pearson correlation, and OLS
multiple regression (SPSS) with t-tests and F-test at 5\% significance.
Collected figures are organized, coded and tabulated before estimation.
Missing annual-report figures are cross-checked against NRB Banking and
Financial Statistics. Variance inflation factors are examined for
multicollinearity and the Durbin--Watson statistic for autocorrelation. The
joint test of all five slopes uses the F-test. Results are reported with
coefficient estimates, standard errors, t-statistics and p-values for every
variable.

Analysis plan: Step 1: descriptive statistics. Step 2: correlation matrix.
Step 3: simple regressions per variable. Step 4: multiple regression
(Eq.~\ref{eq:main}). Step 5: hypothesis tests, interpretation and summary for
Chapters IV--V.
