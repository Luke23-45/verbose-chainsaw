\section{Hypotheses}

The reviewed evidence does not support a uniform directional prediction for
all four measures, particularly for DPS and ROA. Each hypothesis concerns the
corresponding regression coefficient, holding the other included accounting
measures constant. Consistent with the stated two-tailed tests, the alternative
hypotheses are non-directional:

\noindent H1: EPS is significantly associated with $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$
($\beta_1 \ne 0$).

\noindent H2: DPS is significantly associated with $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$
($\beta_2 \ne 0$).

\noindent H3: BVPS is significantly associated with $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$
($\beta_3 \ne 0$).

\noindent H4: ROA is significantly associated with $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$
($\beta_4 \ne 0$).
