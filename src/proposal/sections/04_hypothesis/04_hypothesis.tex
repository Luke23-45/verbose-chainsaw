\section{Hypotheses}

Based on accounting valuation arguments and the mixed empirical evidence
reviewed below, the study expects positive conditional associations in the
multiple regression model. Each hypothesis concerns the corresponding
regression coefficient, holding the other included accounting measures
constant. The
statistical test is two-tailed: the null hypothesis is that the coefficient
equals zero, and the expected positive sign is assessed separately from
statistical significance.

\noindent H1: The conditional association between earnings per share (EPS) and
$\mathit{MPS}^{post}$ is expected to be positive ($\beta_1>0$).

\noindent H2: The conditional association between dividend per share (DPS) and
$\mathit{MPS}^{post}$ is expected to be positive ($\beta_2>0$).

\noindent H3: The conditional association between book value per share (BVPS)
and $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$ is expected to be positive ($\beta_3>0$).

\noindent H4: The conditional association between return on assets (ROA) and
$\mathit{MPS}^{post}$ is expected to be positive ($\beta_4>0$).
