\section{Hypothesis (if any)}

The following hypotheses are formulated based on the theoretical expectations
and empirical evidence discussed in the literature:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $H_1$: There is a significant positive relationship between EPS and MPS.
  \item $H_2$: There is a significant positive relationship between DPS and MPS.
  \item $H_3$: There is a significant positive relationship between P/E ratio and MPS.
  \item $H_4$: There is a significant positive relationship between ROA and MPS.
  \item $H_5$: There is a significant positive relationship between BVPS and MPS.
\end{itemize}
