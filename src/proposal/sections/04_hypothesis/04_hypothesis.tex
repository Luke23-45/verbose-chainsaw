\section{Hypotheses}

The reviewed evidence does not support a uniform directional prediction across
the four measures, particularly for DPS, BVPS, and ROA. Each hypothesis
concerns the corresponding regression coefficient, holding the other included
accounting measures constant. The study therefore uses non-directional
alternative hypotheses. For each coefficient, the null hypothesis is that the
coefficient equals zero; the alternative is that it differs from zero. All
coefficient tests are two-tailed at the 5\% significance level.

\noindent H1: EPS is significantly associated with $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$
($\beta_1 \ne 0$).

\noindent H2: DPS is significantly associated with $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$
($\beta_2 \ne 0$).

\noindent H3: BVPS is significantly associated with $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$
($\beta_3 \ne 0$).

\noindent H4: ROA is significantly associated with $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$
($\beta_4 \ne 0$).
