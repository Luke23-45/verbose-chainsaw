\section{Hypothesis}

Based on accounting valuation arguments and the mixed empirical evidence
reviewed below, the study expects positive conditional associations in the
multiple regression model. Each hypothesis concerns the corresponding
regression coefficient, holding the other included accounting measures
constant. For each coefficient, the statistical test is two-tailed: the null
hypothesis is that the coefficient equals zero, and the expected positive sign
is assessed separately from statistical significance.
\begin{itemize}
  \item $H_1$: The conditional association between earnings per share (EPS) and
    $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$ is expected to be positive ($\beta_1>0$).
  \item $H_2$: The conditional association between dividend per share (DPS) and
    $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$ is expected to be positive ($\beta_2>0$).
  \item $H_3$: The conditional association between book value per share (BVPS)
    and $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$ is expected to be positive ($\beta_3>0$).
  \item $H_4$: The conditional association between return on assets (ROA) and
    $\mathit{MPS}^{post}$ is expected to be positive ($\beta_4>0$).
\end{itemize}
