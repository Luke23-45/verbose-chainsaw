# Proposal Report — Final Formatting Guidelines

**Source (sole authority for every number below):**
`docs/reference/proposal/mbs-dissertation-proposal-sample_1.pdf` (12 pages)
*MBS Dissertation Proposal sample — "Impact of Credit Performance on the Profitability of Commercial Banks in Nepal".*

All values were measured programmatically from the PDF (page geometry, span fonts, font
sizes, line coordinates, indent positions). pt = typographic points (72 pt = 1 inch = 25.4 mm).
Where the sample contains a defect, it is flagged in §16 — do not copy defects.

- Everything is Times New Roman. (A stray empty Calibri 11 pt run at the top-left of every
  page is an empty header artifact — ignore it.)
- **Line spacing: 1.5 lines everywhere in the body** (measured baseline pitch 20.6–20.7 pt
  for 12 pt text = 1.5 × single). No double spacing, no single spacing in body.
- **Paragraph spacing: +10 pt after each paragraph** (paragraph-to-paragraph gap measured
  30.7 pt = one 1.5-line + 10 pt). No first-line indent on any paragraph.
- Body alignment: **fully justified** (both left and right edges flush).

## 3. Title page (page 1) — exact vertical structure

All lines **centered**. Blank lines on the title page are empty paragraphs.

1. **Title** — 18 pt bold, wraps to 2 lines, first line top at y ≈ 72 pt (25.4 mm from top).
   *e.g.* `Impact of Credit Performance on the Profitability of Commercial Banks in Nepal`
2. Two blank lines (18 pt)
3. **`A Dissertation Proposal`** — 18 pt bold, **underlined** (y ≈ 228 pt)
4. Four blank lines
5. **`Submitted by`** — 14 pt (y ≈ 327 pt)
6. `... … … Name … … …` — 12 pt
7. `T U Rgd. No. … … …` — 12 pt
8. `Campus Roll No. … … …` — 12 pt
9. `Exam Roll No. … … …` — 12 pt
10. Four blank lines
11. **`Submitted to`** — 14 pt (y ≈ 520 pt)
12. `Research Department` — 12 pt
13. `…… …… …… …… Campus/College` — 12 pt
14. Two dotted placeholder lines — 12 pt
15. Six blank lines
16. **`Month, Year`** — 14 pt (y ≈ 721 pt)
17. **`Place`** — 14 pt (y ≈ 740 pt)

Notes:
- Order is fixed: **Title → "A Dissertation Proposal" → Submitted by (student block) →
  Submitted to (department/campus block) → Month, Year / Place**.
- "Submitted by" comes **before** "Submitted to" (unlike a full thesis cover).
- There is **no** supervisor name, no "in partial fulfillment", no degree line, no
  approval/signature block on the proposal cover.

## 4. Table of Contents (page 2)

- Own page. Heading **`Table of Contents`** — 16 pt regular (NOT bold), **centered**
  (y ≈ 72 pt).
- The sample leaves the entry area blank. Fill it with the numbered sections 1–7 plus
  `References`, with right-aligned page numbers, in 12 pt TNR.

- Sections run continuously — **no page break before any section**. A new section may start
  mid-page (e.g. §3 starts mid-page 4; §4 and §5 share page 5).
- Heading text starts at the left margin; number format is `1.` / `7.1` (number, period,
  one space) — **no underline, not centered**.
- §7 contains sub-headings: `7.1 Research design`, `7.2 Population and sampling procedure`,
  `7.3 Nature and sources of data collection`, `7.4 Research framework and definition of
  variables`, `7.5 Methods of analysis` — same 14 pt bold as level-1 headings.
- Unnumbered **bold 12 pt** labels inside 7.4/7.5: `Dependent variables`, `Independent
  variables`, `Models`, and each variable definition lead-in
  (`Return on assets (ROA):`, `Credit deposit ratio (CDR):`, …).
- `References` heading: 14 pt bold, **centered** (x-center = text-block center).

## 6. Spacing around headings (measured)

| Transition | Gap (top-of-upper to top-of-lower) |
|---|---|
| L1 heading → body text | 34.1 pt (≈ 1 blank line) |
| Body/list → L1 heading | 21–28 pt (≈ 1 blank line) |
| L2 heading (7.x) → body | 23.5–24.0 pt |
| Body → L2 heading | ≈ 18 pt (≈ 1 blank line) |

Practical recipe: insert **one blank line before every numbered heading**; after L1
headings leave ~1 blank line, after L2 headings a smaller gap (no blank line, default
10 pt space-after).

## 7. Lists

**Roman-numeral lists** (research questions in §2, specific objectives in §3):

- Numbering: `I.`, `II.`, `III.` (uppercase Roman + period).
- Number is **right-aligned** so `I.` starts at x ≈ 112.8 pt, `III.` at x ≈ 104.9 pt
  (right edge of all numbers ≈ 123.2 pt ≈ 30 pt from left margin).
- **List text starts at x = 137.8 pt = 44.2 pt (≈ 1.57 cm / 0.61 in) from the left
  margin**; wrapped lines align at the same 137.8 pt (hanging indent).
- List items use body formatting: 12 pt, 1.5 spacing, justified.
- Items are separated by the normal 1.5-line pitch (no extra blank line between items).
- Lead-in sentence ends with a colon; first item starts on the next line.

**Bullet lists** (rationale bullets in §5):

- Bullet glyph: Wingdings square/arrow bullet (Word's standard ■ list bullet, U+F0A7).
- Bullet position: x = 111.7 pt = **18 pt (0.25 in)** from left margin.
- Text position: x = 129.6 pt = **36 pt (0.5 in)** from left margin (hanging).
- This is exactly Word's default bullet list indent (0.25 in / 0.5 in).

## 8. Hypotheses (§4)

- One lead-in paragraph, then one paragraph per hypothesis.
- Format: `H1: <statement>.` — label `H1:` then statement as a normal 12 pt paragraph,
  left-aligned at margin, 1.5 spacing.
- Numbering must be **H1, H2, H3** (one per independent variable).
- Each hypothesis is a separate paragraph with the standard +10 pt paragraph gap.

## 9. Framework figure & captions (§7.4)

- One schematic diagram (DV box on left, IV boxes on right, labels "Dependent Variable" /
  "Independent Variables" under the boxes) — drawn with Word shapes/text boxes, 12 pt labels.
- Figure sits between the sentence `This study will be based on the schematic diagram as
  presented in Figure 1.` and the caption.
- **Caption below the figure**, 11 pt **italic**, left-aligned at the bullet-text indent
  (x = 111.7 pt ≈ 18 pt in), pattern:
  `Figure 1. Schematic diagram of the theoretical framework.`

## 10. Variable definitions (§7.4)

Structure, in order:

1. Bold 12 pt label: `Dependent variables`
2. One 12 pt justified paragraph explaining the role
3. For each variable: paragraph whose **lead-in term is bold**, definition follows in
   regular weight, same paragraph:
   `Return on assets (ROA): Return on assets is an indicator of …`
4. Same pattern for `Independent variables` and each IV.

Variable name in the bold lead-in uses full form + abbreviation in parentheses
(`Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR):`).

## 11. Models & equations (§7.5, "Models")

- Label paragraph `Models` — bold 12 pt.
- One intro paragraph ending with `… the regression equation will base on the model specified as:`
- **Basic model line** — 12 pt, left-aligned at margin, with the dependent variable symbol
  in *italics*, real subscripts:
  `Y = β₀ + β Xᵢₜ + εᵢₜ`
  (`Y` italic; `0`, `it` are true Word subscripts ≈ 8 pt; β, ε typed as Unicode Greek)
- "Where …" explanation paragraph (12 pt, with true subscripts for β₀ etc.).
- **Estimated equations** — one per dependent variable, 12 pt, left-aligned, one per line,
  no blank line between them, true subscripts:
  `EPSᵢₜ = β₀ + β₁CDRᵢₜ + β₂NPLᵢₜ + β₃CARᵢₜ + εᵢₜ`
  `ROAᵢₜ = β₀ + β₁CDRᵢₜ + β₂NPLᵢₜ + β₃CARᵢₜ + εᵢₜ`
- Then `Where,` on its own line, then one line per symbol definition
  (`EPSit represents earning per share of bank i in year t;` … `β0 is the intercept …`),
  with `i` and `t` italicised where they index banks/years.
- Equations are **not** numbered and **not** centered in the sample.

## 12. In-text citations

- APA author–date: narrative `(Rose, 2002)` / `Kithinji (2010)` / `Bhattarai (2016)`.
- Every work cited in text appears in References and vice versa.

## 13. References

- Heading `References`: 14 pt bold, **centered**, on its own page (last page).
- Entries: 12 pt TNR, **hanging indent 36 pt (0.5 in / 1.27 cm)** — first line at margin
  (93.6 pt), continuation lines at 129.6 pt.
- 1.5 line spacing within an entry; **≈ 6 pt extra between entries** (entry-to-entry gap
  26.6–26.7 pt).
- Alphabetical by author surname.
- APA-style patterns (as used in sample):
  - Journal: `Author, I. (Year). Article title. Journal Name, volume(issue), pages. https://doi.org/…`
  - Book: `Author, I. (Year). Title of Book. City: Publisher.`
  - Web: `Author. (n.d.). Title. Site. URL`
- Journal/book titles **italic**; article/chapter titles roman.
- Sample lists 8 entries for a ~3,000-word proposal — expect roughly 8–15.

## 14. Length & pagination

- **Total: 12 pages** in the sample (1 title + 1 TOC + 9 body + 1 references).
- Body sections (pp. 3–11): **≈ 2,990 words**. Target ≈ 2,800–3,200 words of body text.
- Per-section scale (paragraph/item counts in the sample):
  - §1 Background: 3 paragraphs
  - §2 Problem: 3 paragraphs + 3 Roman-numbered research questions
  - §3 Objectives: 1 lead-in + 3 Roman-numbered objectives
  - §4 Hypotheses: 1 lead-in + 3 hypotheses

  - §7 Methodology: intro + 7.1 (1 para) + 7.2 (1 para) + 7.3 (2 paras) + 7.4 (figure,

  - References: 8 entries

## 16. Defects in the sample — do NOT copy

1. **Hypothesis numbering:** the sample labels the third hypothesis `H1` again
   (H1, H2, **H1**). Use H1, H2, **H3**.
2. **Name mismatch:** in-text `Kithinji (2010)` vs reference entry `Kinthinji, A. M. (2010)`
   — spelling differs between text and list. Keep author spelling identical.
3. **Year typos:** `fiscal year 2015/016 to 2019/020` (should be 2015/16 … 2019/20).
4. **Double period:** `… represents the error term..` — single period only.
5. **Empty TOC:** the sample's Table of Contents page has no entries — fill yours.
6. **Inconsistent space after heading numbers:** `2.  Statement` has two spaces,
   `4. Hypotheses` has one — use exactly one space.
7. **Hypothesis italic glitch:** in the sample the first line of each hypothesis renders
   italic and the second line roman (accidental). Keep hypotheses entirely roman.
