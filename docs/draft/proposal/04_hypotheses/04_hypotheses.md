## Draft

The following hypotheses are formulated for testing, each stated in terms of the
relationship between a firm-specific factor and market price per share.

| No. | Variable pair | Hypothesis |
|-----|---------------|------------|
| H1 | EPS → MPS | There is a positive relationship between earnings per share and market price per share. |
| H2 | DPS → MPS | There is a positive relationship between dividend per share and market price per share. |
| H3 | P/E → MPS | There is a positive relationship between the price-earnings ratio and market price per share. |
| H4 | ROA → MPS | There is a positive relationship between return on assets and market price per share. |
| H5 | BVPS → MPS | There is a positive relationship between book value per share and market price per share. |

Each hypothesis is tested at the 5 percent level of significance using the
`t`-statistic, with the null hypothesis being that the population coefficient
on the relevant factor is zero.
