# Objectives of the Study

> Guideline position: **iii.** (p. 7). General objective in one or two sentences
> as a paragraph, then specific objectives in roman numerals. Use only action
> verbs that can actually be performed: examine, analyse, assess, compare,
> identify, determine, measure. Avoid "to know", "to see", "to understand".

## Draft

The general objective of this study is to examine the factors that determine the
market price of shares of Nepali commercial banks, using contemporary data and
a sample drawn from the banking sector as it is currently constituted.

The specific objectives of the study are:

i. To investigate the relationship between earnings per share, dividend per
   share, price-earnings ratio, book value per share, return on assets and
   market price per share.

ii. To examine the effect of earnings per share, dividend per share,
   price-earnings ratio, book value per share and return on assets on market
   price per share.
