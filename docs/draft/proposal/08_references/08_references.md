> and Thesis Writing*, for citation and referencing format.

## Format standard

APA 7th edition. Every source cited in the body must appear here, and every
entry here must be cited in the body. Alphabetical by author surname.

### Journal article

> Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (2016). Title of the article. *Title of the
> Journal in Title Case*, *Volume*(Issue), page–page range.
> https://doi.org/xxxxx

Note the ampersand before the final author, not "and".

### Book

> Author, A. A. (2016). *Title of the work in sentence case* (2nd ed.). Publisher.

### Government report

> Nepal Rastra Bank. (2023). *Title of report*. Author.

Author is the same as publisher, so the publisher name is omitted.

### Web source

> Organisation. (2024, Month Day). *Title of page*. Site Name. https://url

## Outstanding citation problems

1. **The earlier thesis cites roughly 20 sources but omits several from the
   reference list**, including Lama (2016) and Pradhan (2017), both of which
   appear in the meta table with substantive findings attached. A source cannot
   be summarised in a table and then be absent from the references.
2. **Inconsistent volume and issue data.** Several entries give volume but no
   issue, or neither.
3. **Year inconsistency for one source.** Mosley and Singer is cited in text as
   "(Mosley & Singer, 2018)" in one place and listed in the bibliography as 2008.
   Resolve which is correct.
4. **Bhattarai year conflict.** Cited as 2014 in the literature review and as
   2016 in the background and problem statement, with bibliography entry 2014.
5. **"Thapa & Rana" appears as 2013 in the background and 2011 in the
   bibliography.**
6. **Capitalisation is inconsistent**, particularly for journal titles.
7. **No Nepali-language sources are cited at all**, despite the NEPSE, NRB and
   Ministry of Finance data being Nepali-language publications. A Nepali thesis
   drawing on domestic institutional sources should engage the Nepali
   literature, including the Nepalese Business Studies journal and NRB working
   paper series.
8. **No sources after 2016** except Shrestha and Subedi (2014). The review will
   read as dated.
