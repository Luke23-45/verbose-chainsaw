# 10 — Sentence templates for this thesis (Phrasebank method)

Method source: Manchester Academic Phrasebank
(https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk — Introducing work, Describing
methods, Reporting results, Discussing findings, Writing conclusions;
John Morley's compiled editions). The Phrasebank's insight: academic
writing uses recurring rhetorical frames, and writers (especially in a
second language) produce better prose by filling proven frames than by
improvising.

This file adapts the frames to THIS thesis — every template is filled
with the thesis's real variables, numbers, and institutions, so any new
paragraph built from them is voice-consistent by construction. When
drafting new text, copy the closest template and change only the
bracketed slot.

---

## 1. Introducing work / establishing territory (Ch. I, §2.2–2.4)

- "A central question in [financial economics and accounting valuation]
  is whether [the empirical relationship between published financial
  statement numbers and equity market prices remains stable across
  structural corporate reorganizations]." — the thesis's opening frame.
- "Among competing [valuation architectures], the [residual income
  valuation] framework pioneered by [Ohlson (1995)] provides ..."
- "Empirical studies ... have provided extensive documentation across
  [developed, emerging, and frontier] equity markets
  \citep{...}." — Move 1(b) template.
- "In [frontier] market environments, however, [the informational
  capacity of equity markets] is frequently challenged by [structural
  friction, regulatory evolution, ...]." — the pivot toward the niche.

## 2. Establishing the niche (Ch. I §1.2, Ch. II §2.5)

- "However, [no empirical study identified in the literature reviewed in
  Chapter II] has [formally evaluated whether the valuation coefficients
  of EPS and BVPS shifted between ...]."
- "A common practice across studies reviewed in Chapter II is [the
  inclusion of the price-earnings (P/E) ratio alongside fundamental
  accounting variables in regression models where MPS serves as the
  dependent variable] \citep{...}."
- "To address these empirical, methodological, and econometric problems,
  this study investigates [three] central research questions:"
- Concession + rebuttal: "While recent work by [Gurung and Dahal (2025)]
  introduces [bank fixed effects]..., almost all existing studies
  estimate [static, full-period average coefficients]."

## 3. Describing methods (Ch. III)

- "This study adopts a [quantitative, correlational, and comparative
  longitudinal panel] research design using [secondary financial
  accounting and capital market data]."
- "Purposive sampling is conceptually appropriate because [the core
  research objective focuses specifically on ...], while requiring
  [longitudinal data continuity across both pre- and post-merger
  regimes]."
- Rule template: "If [a security was suspended from trading or recorded
  zero valid transactions] within [a five-day calendar window following
  the study's operationalized reporting deadline], the observation is
  coded as missing and excluded from the regression sample. It is never
  imputed from [stale pre-suspension prices]." — the
  condition/protocol/sanction shape; reuse for any new data rule.
- Choice/alternative/rejection template: "To ensure [conservative,
  reliable inference], [standard cluster-robust covariance estimators]
  ... suffer from [severe downward bias ... when the number of clusters
  is small (G = 5)] \citep{cameron2008clusters}. [Standard errors,
  t-statistics, and confidence intervals] are therefore computed using
  [MacKinnon-White HC3] ... referred to [Student's t distribution on
  residual degrees of freedom]."

## 4. Reporting results (Ch. IV §4.2)

- Table-anchored report: "[The baseline results in] Table~\ref{...}
  provide ... [The model exhibits a high coefficient of determination
  ($R^2 = 0.948$...)]. However, ... this high explanatory power is
  almost entirely driven by [the two-way fixed effects structure]."
- Point-and-interval: "For [EPS], the $t$-statistic is $[0.51]$ ($p =
  [0.616]$ on $t([16])$), and the 95\% HC3 confidence interval spans
  from $[-6.587]$ to $[10.777]$."
- Economic magnitude: "In economic terms, this suggests that each
  additional [NPR 1.00] of [reported earnings per share] is associated
  with an incremental [NPR 2.10] in [post-deadline market share price],
  holding [unobserved bank characteristics and common macro-temporal
  shocks] constant."
- Regime narrative: "[Prior] to [the 2023 consolidation wave], the
  estimated valuation slope for [earnings] was $[\beta_E = 5.577]$ ...
  while [the book value slope] was [negligible] ... The estimated
  post-consolidation shift parameters imply [compressed] slopes of ..."
- Robustness: "Substituting [ROA] for [EPS] yields ... [2.46]
  ($p = [0.121]$). This [confirms] that the observed valuation dynamics
  are not an artifact of [per-share earnings scaling]." (Note: "confirms"
  is licensed here because R3 tests a specification artifact, not a
  hypothesis — restrict this verb to that use.)

## 5. Discussing findings (Ch. IV §4.3)

- Agreement frame: "In line with [Kwon and Wang (2020)], our findings
  are consistent with the perspective that [corporate amalgamations can
  introduce accounting noise] ..."
- Divergence frame: "Where our results diverge: in [Kwon and Wang's]
  framework, [book value typically assumes an anchoring role] ... In our
  [Nepalese commercial banking] sample, [book value] conspicuously did
  not serve as [an asset anchor] ... Unlike [non-financial
  corporations], [commercial banks] cannot be readily evaluated on an
  orderly liquidation basis because [they are subject to statutory
  resolution regimes]."
- Mechanism frame: "[Asset Quality Pressures and Loan Provisioning]
  (Plausible Mechanism): ... This mechanism is consistent with [the
  observed drop in the earnings multiple], though [direct asset quality
  variables are not estimated] in the econometric model."
- Comparative frame: "Prior empirical literature on [Nepalese commercial
  banks] has predominantly reported [large, statistically significant
  positive coefficients] \citep{...}. In contrast, our [two-way fixed
  effects] baseline yields [statistically insignificant slopes] ...
  This divergence reflects [four] critical methodological factors:"
- Self-limit frame: "[The negative implied slope] is statistically
  insignificant under the [Student's $t(14)$] distribution. It cannot
  therefore be presented as an established empirical law."

## 6. Writing conclusions (Ch. V)

- Answer frame: "Answer to Research Question [1] ([Baseline Value
  Relevance]): [Fundamental accounting measures ... do not exhibit a
  statistically significant association ...] ($p = 0.616$ for EPS; $p =
  0.908$ for BVPS)."
- Decision frame: "Because both $p$-values exceed the [5]% significance
  threshold, the study fails to reject the null hypothesis [H_0].
  [Accounting earnings] do not exhibit a statistically significant
  association with [post-reporting-deadline market prices] once
  [macro-temporal shocks are absorbed]."
- Sensitivity frame: "The evidence for [a structural valuation regime
  shift] is therefore [sensitivity-dependent] rather than conclusive in
  the baseline; it is documented as [an empirical association across
  calendar periods], rather than as [a causal merger impact]."
- Implication frame (each §5.4 item): "[Finding-grounding clause: The
  sharp reduction in cash dividend distributions post-consolidation
  removed a historical valuation anchor] ... [NRB] should [provide
  clear, forward-looking guidelines regarding ...], helping [market
  participants formulate realistic cash flow projections]." — the
  two-part shape: finding clause, then actor + action + purpose.
- Future-work frame: "Future studies should [expand the cross-sectional
  sample to encompass all 20 surviving commercial banks on NEPSE],
  employing [difference-in-differences panel frameworks] that compare
  [aggressively merged banking series] against [a larger cohort of
  organically grown peer institutions]."

## 7. Limitations frames (Ch. I §1.6, Ch. V)

- Scope/limitation separation: "The study's scope is confined to [five
  purposively selected Class 'A' commercial bank series observed across
  six fiscal years]. ... [Findings apply directly to the selected
  institutions and their specific consolidation contexts]." — states the
  scope, then the limitation inside it.
- Protocol limitation: "While [in the disclosures examined for this
  study, commercial bank boards typically released audited highlights
  between Ashwin and Poush], this pattern reflects observed corporate
  practice rather than audited proof of disclosure timing for every
  individual observation."
- Unobservability frame: "[Any unrecorded informal information leakage
  prior to public board announcements] remains an unobservable
  institutional feature of [frontier equity markets]."

## 8. Anti-slop rule for using these templates

A template may be filled only with content that already exists in the
thesis (its numbers, its citations, its institutions) or is sourced
afresh with a citation. Never let a template generate a new factual
claim — the frame is rhetoric, the slot is evidence. If no real content
fills a slot, the paragraph should not exist.
