```
5. Rationale of the study
```
Para:
```
The structural consolidation of the Nepalese banking sector, through the merger wave of 2013–2020 and the major consolidation of early 2023, fundamentally reshaped the structure and capital base of commercial banks (Adhikari et al., 2023a, 2023b). Following extensive mergers and share swaps, secondary market prices also experienced substantial downward adjustments (Adhikari et al., 2023a, 2023b). Investigating how accounting fundamentals relate to equity valuation across this transitional period offers meaningful insights for multiple stakeholders: for investors and analysts tracking market valuations against reported fundamentals, for bank management evaluating market responses to financial results and dividend policies, for regulatory bodies including Nepal Rastra Bank and the Securities Board of Nepal monitoring secondary market dynamics across the consolidation window, and for future academic research through a baseline specification that addresses the ROA and BVPS contradictions without definitional regressor circularity.
```
