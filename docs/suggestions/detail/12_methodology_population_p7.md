Para:
```
The target population of the study comprises all 20 commercial banks operating in Nepal as of mid-2023 (Dangol & Shrestha, 2024), following the extensive regulatory consolidation that reduced the sector from a peak of 32 commercial banks (Adhikari et al., 2023b). From this population, a sample of 5 commercial banks is selected using a purposive (judgmental) sampling technique. The selection criteria require: (i) representation of the prominent commercial bank mergers that defined the early 2023 consolidation wave, (ii) inclusion of an unmerged comparison bank (NMB Bank) to provide comparative balance, and (iii) continuous availability of audited annual financial statements and NEPSE trading records across the entire study period. Because the sample is non-probabilistic and comprises 5 institutions, the empirical findings are interpreted specifically for the sampled banks and serve as an indicative benchmark rather than an automatic generalization across all 20 commercial banks.
```
