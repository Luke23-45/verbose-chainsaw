For merged banks, pre-merger observations represent the acquiring institution, whereas post-merger observations represent the consolidated institution. Consequently, the merger alters the economic scale and composition of the bank-level observation, and this study does not interpret regression coefficients as direct estimates of the causal effect of the mergers themselves. The study covers a six-year observation period from FY 2076/77 to FY 2081/82, spanning the period before, during, and after the major 2023 consolidation of Nepal's commercial banking sector, yielding 30 bank-year observations (5 sampled bank series × 6 fiscal years = 30 observations).

The research framework illustrating the functional relationship between the explanatory accounting fundamentals and the dependent variable is depicted in Figure 1.
