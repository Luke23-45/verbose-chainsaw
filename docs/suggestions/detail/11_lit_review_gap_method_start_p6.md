```
The existing literature exhibits three key limitations. First, most studies are confined to periods ending prior to the major 2023 consolidation wave, leaving valuation behavior across the recent restructuring unexamined (Adhikari et al., 2023b; Chaudhari, 2025). Second, empirical results on ROA and BVPS are directly contradictory: Chhetri (2023) reports ROA as a significant positive correlate, whereas Chaudhari (2025) reports it as significantly negative, and Gyawali (2022) as positive but insignificant. On BVPS, Joshi et al. (2023) find it significant while Pant et al. (2024) find it negative and insignificant. Third, several earlier specifications include P/E as an explanatory variable alongside MPS (Y. R. Bhattarai, 2014; Chhetri, 2023). Because the P/E ratio is by definition calculated as MPS/EPS, the present study excludes P/E from the regression specification to avoid direct mathematical dependence between the dependent and explanatory variables.
```
